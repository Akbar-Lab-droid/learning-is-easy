# Learning Is Easy with Aiproxcrop

A free learning hub for international students — books, YouTube lessons, proven study methods, and a clear map of what comes after your bachelor's degree.

**Live website:** https://akbar-lab-droid.github.io/learning-is-easy/

## What's inside

- **10 essential books** — from *Deep Work* and *Atomic Habits* to *Make It Stick* and free OpenStax textbooks.
- **10 YouTube channels** — CrashCourse, Khan Academy, TED-Ed, Thomas Frank, Ali Abdaal, MIT OpenCourseWare, freeCodeCamp, and more.
- **8 study methods** — Pomodoro, Active Recall, Spaced Repetition, Feynman Technique, Cornell Notes, Mind Mapping, SQ3R, and Interleaving, with a filter by goal.
- **8 paths after graduation** — Master's, PhD & research, jobs, internships, startups, freelancing, teaching, and working abroad.
- **International student starter checklist** — visa, housing, bank, SIM, health insurance, orientation, and community — with progress saved in your browser.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder with any static server. No build step, no dependencies — just HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript.

## Link checker

All external links are checked with `check-links.py`:

```bash
python3 check-links.py
```
